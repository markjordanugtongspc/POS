Chapter 01

Opening Procedure

Prepare the store terminal before the first sale.

Begin with a clean terminal state. Sign in with the assigned account, confirm the date and time, and verify the drawer balance before selling.

  • Device check: connect receipt printer, barcode scanner, and cash drawer.
  • Money check: count beginning cash and record the amount in the shift note.
  • Sync check: confirm no offline logs are pending before opening.

Chapter 03

Inventory Control

How stock should move from receiving to checkout.

Use Products for item setup, SKU control, category grouping, and low-stock review. Never create duplicate SKUs for the same sellable item.

When receiving deliveries, update quantity first, then price. If cost changes, record the reason in the product notes before saving.

EventOwner Action
Low stockReview supplier and reorder before threshold reaches zero.
Out of stockDisable item from sale or mark unavailable.
Price changeUpdate product and verify checkout price.
