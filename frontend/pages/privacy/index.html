Privacy Center

Privacy Policy

Jorgy POS keeps store operations private while allowing the system creator/administrator to manage the operational records needed to support the store: contact support logs, uploaded screenshots and files, ticket messages, sales records, transactions, products, users, and settings. Passwords and other authentication secrets are stored hashed in the cloud and are not readable as plain text.

Section 01

Scope and administrator visibility

Owner readable

This policy explains what information the Jorgy POS system handles and what the developer/creator of the system may access through the administrator portal. The administrator portal is used to support the store, diagnose problems, manage operational records, and resolve issues reported by the owner or staff.

The creator/administrator may manage support logs, uploaded screenshots and files, ticket messages, product records, sales records, transaction history, user metadata, and system settings. Passwords are not available as readable plain text because authentication secrets are hashed and kept through the cloud provider.

Store operations

Sales, receipts, products, stock, transaction history, and reports can be managed for support and operations.

Staff access

Owner, inventory manager, cashier, roles, account status, and related non-password account metadata.

Support

Contact support logs, messages, screenshots, uploaded files, notes, and diagnostics.

Section 02

Data the creator/admin can access and manage

The administrator portal is intentionally operational. It may show the data needed to keep the system working, answer support requests, verify sales behavior, repair records, and investigate issues. It is not intended to expose passwords or readable authentication secrets.

Transaction data

Receipt number, items sold, quantities, VAT, discount, payment method, cashier, timestamps, refund or void status, and other sales or transaction records.

Product and stock data

SKU, product names, category, brand, price, quantity, thresholds, product images, and stock adjustments.

Staff account data

Name, email, initials, role, status, quota or activity indicators, and permission assignments. Password values are hashed and are not readable.

Support data

Ticket subject, category, priority, contact support messages, notes, uploaded screenshots, file attachments, device details, and resolution history.

Data the creator/admin cannot read as plain text

Account passwords and similar authentication secrets are hashed before storage. Hashing is designed so the raw password is not readable by the developer, owner, staff, or database viewer.

Section 03

How administrator access is used

Operational data is used to process checkout, calculate totals, print receipts, update stock, display dashboards, export reports, assign staff responsibilities, and resolve support issues.

The creator/administrator may inspect contact support logs, screenshots, uploaded files, ticket messages, sales records, transaction records, product records, and account metadata when providing support or maintaining the platform.

Support notes and uploaded files should only include what is needed to explain the problem. Avoid sending private customer information, personal IDs, passwords, bank details, or unrelated screenshots.

Exported CSV files, printed receipts, screenshots, and downloaded records become owner-controlled records. Store them securely and limit access to trusted staff only.

Section 04

Security and password protection

Recommended owner controls
  • Use separate accounts for every cashier.
  • Review roles after staff changes.
  • Lock the terminal when the counter is unattended.
  • Never share the owner password unless the person is truly trusted.
Cloud and password handling
  • Passwords are stored as hashes, not readable text.
  • Sensitive authentication data is kept through the cloud service.
  • The developer cannot read the original password from the hash.
  • Reset compromised passwords immediately.

Section 06

Owner responsibility and controls

The owner is responsible for the store's data, account sharing decisions, staff permissions, exported reports, uploaded files, and any information entered into support tickets. The safest practice is simple: never share the owner account password with anyone unless that person is truly trusted and authorized to control the store.

Owners can review account access, export operational reports, correct product and staff records, clear local cache, and request support for suspected data problems. Where customer privacy laws apply, owners should handle access or deletion requests according to their store policy and local legal requirements.

Access

Review records and exports available to owner accounts.

Correct

Update inaccurate products, users, store settings, or ticket notes.

Restrict

Disable accounts and remove access for staff who no longer need it.

Export

Download reports for bookkeeping and operational review.

Section 07

Contact and updates

For privacy questions, support tickets, device concerns, or suspected unauthorized access, use Contact Support in the sidebar and include the terminal name, affected user, date, and a short description. This policy may be updated as features, integrations, and owner controls evolve.
